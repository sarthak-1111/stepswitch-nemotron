Nebius x NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon · Coding and Agentic Engineering

The right Nemotron for every step of a coding task.

Most of a coding agent’s calls do not need the largest model: find a file, read it, make a small edit, run the tests. Stepswitch gives each step to the Nemotron model that fits it on Token Factory. Lightning reads, Super edits and runs your tests, Ultra plans and reviews, and your tests decide what counts as done.
